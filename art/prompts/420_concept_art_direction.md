# 420 Concept Art Prompt

Create cinematic concept art for GEHENNA, a server-authoritative occult simulation set in the Iron Age / Late Bronze Age southern Levant.

Use the early reference images as tonal anchors: a dark codex cover, a cave ritual interface, and a practitioner facing remembered dead figures. Update the image with the clearer game direction: a persistent shared world where ritual sites remember many players, damage can scar but also heal, and spirits are specific dead people rather than monsters.

Scene:

A night ritual site at the Ridge of Elah, above a dry wadi. The mouth of a burial cave opens in pale limestone. Around it are broken pottery, sheep knucklebones/astragali, oil lamps, a bronze spearhead, linen scraps, bone fragments, inscribed potsherds, a shallow water vessel, ash, and old blood in the dust. The stone shows thin red-gold fissures from a prior taboo act, but at the edge of the damaged circle a quiet healer-practitioner kneels with water, herbs, and testimony tablets, trying to repair the site. Farther back, several faint dead figures stand as remembered human presences with distinct bearing and clothing, not demons.

Mood:

Ancient, somber, archaeological, dangerous, quiet, textured. It should feel like a compiled cosmology and a living world record, not spellcasting spectacle.

Composition:

Wide game key art. The viewer is at human height, just outside the ritual boundary. The cave, the healer, the scarred circle, and the watching dead all matter. Oil lamps and moonlight should create readable silhouettes. Include no modern UI, but the arrangement of objects should imply ritual grammar and consequence.

Palette:

Limestone, ash, bronze, dark soil, olive oil flame, parchment, bone, smoke, dried blood, and restrained red-gold fissures.

Negative prompt:

No horned demons, no gothic cathedral, no medieval wizard robes, no glowing spell hands, no clean high-fantasy armor, no superhero poses, no generic hellscape, no modern spell hotbar, no neon magic, no purple-blue fantasy gradient, no action RPG combat framing.
