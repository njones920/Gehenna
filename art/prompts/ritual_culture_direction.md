# Ritual Culture Concept Direction

Create concept art for GEHENNA, a server-authoritative occult simulation set around historically grounded ancient Levantine ritual practice.

Use the early reference images as tonal anchors only: a dark codex cover, a cave ritual interface, and a practitioner facing remembered dead figures. Do not copy the exact scenes. Keep the tone, pressure, and archaeological seriousness while making the people and cult setting feel more like local Canaanite/Baal-associated worshippers than fantasy necromancers.

Historical Visual Gravity:

- local Canaanite/Levantine worshippers, priests, kings, mourners, servants, and household participants
- robes, tunics, wrapped cloaks, kilts or short skirts where appropriate, belts, close caps, cloth head coverings, toggle pins/fibulae, simple jewelry, and rank-specific ceremonial clothing
- altars, stelae, food and drink offerings, animal remains, votive vessels, figurines, oil lamps, potsherds, bronze tools, inscriptions, water, wine, ash, and dust
- Baal/storm/fertility associations suggested through weather, mountain, thunder, vegetation, drought, harvest, and royal protection; do not turn Baal symbols into superhero magic
- the dead as remembered human presences with social identity, not monsters

Mood:

Ancient, somber, archaeological, dangerous, quiet, textured. It should feel like a compiled cosmology and a living world record, not spellcasting spectacle.

Composition:

Prefer human-height viewpoints, ritual proximity, and readable silhouettes. The arrangement of objects should imply ritual grammar and consequence without needing modern UI. Let the image choose its own site: cave, open-air altar, temple court, village threshold, ruined settlement, or tomb.

Palette:

Limestone, ash, bronze, dark soil, olive oil flame, parchment, bone, smoke, dried blood, textile color, storm-dark sky, and drought-yellow vegetation.

Negative prompt:

No horned demons, no gothic cathedral, no medieval wizard robes, no glowing spell hands, no clean high-fantasy armor, no superhero poses, no generic hellscape, no modern spell hotbar, no neon magic, no purple-blue fantasy gradient, no action RPG combat framing.
