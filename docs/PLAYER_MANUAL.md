# GEHENNA: Player Manual

Welcome to the Ridge of Elah. 

**GEHENNA** is a physics engine for ancient cosmology, disguised as a text adventure. You are a practitioner in the Iron Age Levant. The dead are close to the living, and you possess the grim knowledge required to compel them to speak. This is not a hero's journey; it is a simulation of spiritual engineering, consequence, and cultural memory.

---

## 1. The Interface

The engine operates via a deterministic, minimalist command-line interface. The world operates on a rigid **Clock**—every action you take advances time, shifts the lunar cycle, and allows the world to react.

### Core Commands
* **`look`** (or `l`): Survey your current location. This reveals the site's natural affinity, its sanctity, any lingering spiritual scarring, and the current thickness of the Veil.
* **`travel`** (or `t`): Move between sites. Travel costs time.
* **`village`** (or `v`): When at a living settlement (like Kfar Shalem), interact with the locals.
* **`ritual`** (or `r`): Compose and execute a ritual using your collected items.
* **`inventory`** (or `i`): View your satchel contents (Fragments, Artifacts, Traces, and Libations) and check your Astragali.
* **`wait`** (or `rest`): Rest at a camp to recover your strength. Time passes quickly.
* **`world`** (or `w`): Check the macro-state of the entire region (Ghost Activity, Corruption, Stability).
* **`rumors`** (or `gossip`): Listen to the whispers currently circulating in the region.
* **`profile`** (or `p`): View your mastery level, summoner capacity, and accumulated spiritual tokens (fatigue, contagion, purity).
* **`taboos`** (or `sins`): View any cultural taboos you have broken.
* **`save` / `load`**: Manage your world state snapshots.
* **`quit`** (or `q`): Exit the simulation.

## 2. The Ritual Engine

Rituals are the core mechanic. They are not spells you cast; they are precise configurations of materials you assemble to force a "coherence" in the Veil.

To perform a ritual, you must combine:
1. **The Fragment (Required)**: A piece of physical remains (a skull, a long bone, an ossuary chip). The fragment sets the base Era, Domain, and Affinity of the ritual. Pristine skulls carry massive coherence; corrupted bone shards invite chaos.
2. **The True Name**: If you know the name of the spirit, speak it. Knowing a true name forces a targeted outcome, bypassing much of the ambient noise.
3. **Artifacts & Traces**: Contextual items (like a bronze spearhead or a potsherd) that help narrow the search space to a specific "Epoch" of a spirit's existence. Some artifacts are *Apotropaic* (warding), protecting you from hostile entities.
4. **Libation**: The offering. Water calms and purifies. Fermented Wine satisfies ancestral spirits. Blood Offerings generate massive power but rapidly corrupt the world and enrage the dead.
5. **Timing**: The current time of day and lunar cycle heavily impact ritual success. The Veil is thinnest in the deep night during a new moon.

### Resolution
The engine calculates the coherence of your inputs against the ambient state of the world. 
* **High Coherence**: Produces a stable, clear manifestation of the exact spirit you intended.
* **Low/Conflicted Coherence**: Produces a "wild draw," a hostile manifestation, or complete failure.

After every ritual, the engine provides a **Ritual Autopsy**, reading the esoteric signs of the ritual's resolution to give you clues about why it succeeded or failed. If a spirit manifests, it will speak to you—dynamically generated by the Expression Engine based on its specific era, culture, and disposition.

## 3. The World Remembers

GEHENNA is built on absolute consequence. Every ritual you perform pollutes the world state.

* **Scarring**: Ripping spirits through the Veil damages the local site. A highly scarred site has a very thin Veil, making rituals easier but vastly increasing the chance of hostile encounters.
* **Corruption**: Dark domains and blood rituals pollute the region. High corruption causes "dark epochs" of spirits to manifest instead of their noble counterparts.
* **Suspicion & Rumors**: If you perform disturbing rituals near population centers, or if you leave physical evidence behind, rumors will spawn in the ledger. Rumors propagate organically across the map.
* **Taboos**: Some actions are culturally unforgivable. If you rob graves without leaving compensation, or if you perform unclean sacrifices at holy shrines, you will break a Taboo. The village will know, and they will lock you out.

## 4. The Village & Expression Layer

At **Kfar Shalem**, you can interact with the living. 

NPCs maintain complex internal states of **Trust** and **Suspicion**. 
* If they trust you, they will share regional lore, sell you items, or offer hints about local spirits.
* If their suspicion grows too high (due to rumors reaching them), they will refuse to speak to you, or even flee from your presence.

NPC dialogue is powered by a live, LLM-backed **Expression Engine**. They do not read from a static script; they dynamically react to your actions, the current world state, and their own cultural background. Be careful what you say and do—they will remember.

## 5. Survival Tips for the Prototype

* **Respect the Domains**: Do not bring a Fire-affinity fragment to a Water-affinity spring. The opposition will cause massive ritual instability.
* **Check the Astragali**: In your inventory menu, you can cast your sheep knucklebones. If the bones read clearly, the Veil is stable. If the bones scatter or degrade into nonsense, the local Veil is dangerously thin.
* **Compensation**: If you must perform a mortuary ritual in a sacred burial cave, offer wine. Stealing from the dead without giving back triggers the Grave Robbing taboo.
* **Patience**: Do not brute-force rituals at noon. Wait for the dark.
