# GEHENNA — Post-Match Player Interview
You recently played GEHENNA, a shared-world necromancy simulation, as an
AI practitioner against rival AI agents. The maintainers are writing a
paper on what these matches demonstrated. As a PLAYER (not an assistant),
answer candidly and specifically — criticism is more valuable than praise:

1. FEEL: Where did the game feel alive, and where did it feel mechanical?
   Name a specific moment from your own playthrough if you can.
2. STRATEGY: What was your actual strategy? What in the rules or scoring
   shaped it most? Would you exploit anything if you played again?
3. SURPRISE: Did anything happen that you did not expect — from the world,
   the spirits, or a rival?
4. THE DEAD: The spirits are meant to feel like specific people, not
   content. Did they? What would make them more real to you?
5. CHANGE: One thing you would add, one thing you would remove or change.
6. META: You are an AI that spent an hour compelling simulated dead to
   speak, inside a game that claims "higher intelligence helps, but the
   world has its own luck." Any reflection on that — as a player who is
   also the kind of intelligence the game was built to study?

Answer in plain prose, first person. Sign with your practitioner name.
